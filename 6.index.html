Leadership under pressure

Two moments that show how I lead: the challenge, what I did, and what changed.

COVID, 2020–2021

Moving 700 schools online

Offline›Digital content›Online classes›School success

The challenge. Schools closed overnight, and an entire offline academic system across 16 states and 20+ program combinations had to move online at once.

What I did. I led the switch to digital content and online classes, and guided teachers and school leaders through every academic and non-academic change. I also ran content creation, ingestion, delivery, post-delivery support and school-success operations.

The outcome. 700 schools kept teaching online. Portfolio retention improved to about 90% on a US$8M+ (₹80 Cr+) portfolio over this period.

Customer support

Building 3 call-centre programs

Design›Staff and train›Automate›Measure

The challenge. Education programs needed reliable support for schools, parents and students, without support costs growing with every new program.

What I did. I set up three call-centre programs for EdTech and education. I connected ticketing, escalations and SLAs across Zendesk and Lark, then automated call audits and email handling with AI.

The outcome. Support that scales with each new program. See the AI Call Audit and Email Automation case studies for the measured results.
